## What's on this page

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Company name** (black bar) | Your company, as registered in Ordflow. |
| **Branches** | Your company's branches. Most companies have one. **Click its name** to go to the [orders board](../orders/index.md). |
| **Members** | The people who can log in to your company. Click **+** to invite a colleague, and the pencil to edit a member. |
| **House icon** (top left) | Brings you back to this page from anywhere in Ordflow. |
| **Your initials** (top right) | Your user menu, with **Sign out**. |

!!! info "Branches: an advanced feature"
    A company can open more than one branch, for example one in Haifa and one in Jerusalem. Each branch has its own orders and settings.
