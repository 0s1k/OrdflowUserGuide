# Quick start: your first shipment

The whole journey from login to pickup, one step per screen. Each step links to the full explanation.

!!! tip "Before your first shipment"
    Do the [initial setup](setup/index.md) once: VAT number, sender address, presets and connecting your store.

<div class="quick-steps" markdown>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 1. Log in

Go to [app.ordflow.com](https://app.ordflow.com) and sign in with your email and password.

[Logging in →](getting-started/login.md)

![Sign in](assets/img/login.png)

</div>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 2. Open your company

Click your company name under **Branches**.

[After you log in →](getting-started/dashboard.md)

![Start page](assets/img/getting-started/start-screen.jpg)

</div>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 3. Get your orders

Orders from your connected store arrive in **New**. You can also **Import** an Excel file.

[The orders board →](orders/index.md) · [Import from Excel →](orders/import.md)

![Orders board](assets/img/orders/after-import.jpg)

</div>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 4. Select the orders of one service

Tick the orders, or put them in a stage per service and tick the stage. Then click **Ship**.

[Using stages →](orders/index.md#stages) · [Shipping rules →](orders/rules.md)

![Selected order](assets/img/orders/board-selected.jpg)

</div>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 5. Choose a preset and create the labels

Pick the **preset** for that service, check the weight (per item), then click **Create Labels**.

[Create shipping labels →](orders/create-labels.md) · [Express →](orders/express.md)

![Ship panel with a preset](assets/img/orders/ship-panel-usa.jpg){ width="260" }

</div>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 6. Print the labels and finish

Click **Print › Labels**, stick each label on its parcel, then click **Finish**. The shipments move to **Shipments**.

[Create shipping labels →](orders/create-labels.md#5-print-the-labels-and-finish)

![Shipments summary](assets/img/orders/shipments-summary.jpg){ width="260" }

</div>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 7. Print the documents and label the bags

In **Shipments**, filter by courier, select all, then **Print › Air Waybill** (number of bags), **Invoices** and **Manifest**. Put one AWB in a pouch on each bag.

[Shipping documents →](orders/documents.md) · [How to label a bag →](service-guide/bag-labeling.md)

![Print menu](assets/img/orders/docs-print-menu.jpg)

</div>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 8. Send the documents

Upload the 3 files of each service to the documents form. Check the email you get back and **forward it to ops@mailogs.com**.

[Shipping documents →](orders/documents.md#3-send-the-documents)

![Documents form](assets/img/orders/docs-form-top.jpg){ width="260" }

</div>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 9. Order the pickup

In the Center, order a Mailog driver. Outside the Center, email ops@mailogs.com for a DHL pickup. Express pickups are booked with the labels.

[Mailog driver pickup →](service-guide/driver-pickup.md) · [Outside the Center →](service-guide/dhl-pickup.md)

![Pickup form](assets/img/service/pickup-form.jpg){ width="220" }

</div>

<div class="quick-step" markdown>

### 10. Mark the shipments Complete

In **Shipments**, select the shipments you've sent and choose **Actions › Complete**. You're ready for the next batch.

[Reports and history →](orders/reports.md)

</div>

</div>
