# Sender addresses

Your sender address is where your parcels are shipped from and where the courier collects them. You can save several addresses, for example your warehouse and your office.

Sender addresses are in **Settings › Shipments**.

## Add an address

1. Click the **Address Name** field and choose **New Address...**.
2. Click the field again and type a short nickname for the address, for example *Main warehouse*. The nickname is only for you, so you can tell your addresses apart.
3. Fill in the fields marked with a red **\***.
4. Click **Save**. The button turns active once all required fields are filled in.

## Change or delete an address

Choose the address in the **Address Name** list, make your changes and click **Save**, or click **Delete** to remove it.
