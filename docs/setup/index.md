# Initial setup

Do this once, before you ship your first order. It takes about 15 minutes.

## Setup checklist

Work through these in order. Each step links to a page with screenshots.

- [ ] **Check your basic details:** branch name, time zone, currency, units. [General info →](general-info.md)
- [ ] **Enter your VAT number** (מספר עוסק מורשה או ח״פ). [Accounting →](accounting.md#vat-number)
- [ ] **Add the VAT numbers from your sales channels** (for example, Etsy's IOSS number). [Accounting →](accounting.md#vat-types-ioss-and-others)
- [ ] **Make sure Israel is in the country filter** so domestic orders aren't pulled in. [Orders settings →](orders.md)
- [ ] **Add your sender address.** [Sender addresses →](addresses.md)
- [ ] **Create your shipping presets** (USA, EU, Rest of World). [Presets →](presets.md)
- [ ] **Connect your store** (Etsy, Shopify and others). [Integrations →](integrations.md)

Optional:

- [ ] Replace product titles with one customs description. [Accounting →](accounting.md#default-customs-description-optional)
- [ ] Send tracking numbers back to your store automatically. [Shipment settings →](shipment-settings.md#autocomplete-ecommerce-orders)
- [ ] Skip free items like thank-you cards. [Shipment settings →](shipment-settings.md#filter-sku)

## Where to find Settings

All settings are under the **gear icon** at the bottom of the left menu. Settings has four tabs: **General Info**, **Accounting**, **Orders** and **Shipments**. Integrations has its own icon in the left menu.
