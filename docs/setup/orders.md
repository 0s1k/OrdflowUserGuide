**Settings › Orders** controls how your orders page looks and which orders Ordflow pulls in.

## Page view

Choose how the **Orders** page shows your orders: **Stages** or **Grid**.

The change is saved straight away.

=== "Stages"

    Orders are cards arranged in columns (stages). See [The orders board](../orders/index.md#stages).

    ![Stages view](../assets/img/orders/after-import.jpg)

=== "Grid"

    Orders are rows in a table, with title, number of items, ID, SKU, recipient, value and order date.

    ![Grid view](../assets/img/orders/orders-grid.jpg)

## Filter countries

Orders going to the countries in this list are **not pulled into Ordflow** from your sales channels.

!!! tip "Keep Israel in this list"
    Mailog doesn't ship within Israel, so domestic orders should never be pulled into Ordflow. **Israel** should always be in the list.

To add a country, click the field and choose it. To remove one, click the **⊗** next to it.
