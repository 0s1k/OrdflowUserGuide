## Filter countries

Orders going to the countries in this list are **not pulled into Ordflow** from your sales channels.

!!! tip "Keep Israel in this list"
    Mailog doesn't ship within Israel, so domestic orders should never be pulled into Ordflow. **Israel** should always be in the list.

To add a country, click the field and choose it. To remove one, click the **⊗** next to it.
