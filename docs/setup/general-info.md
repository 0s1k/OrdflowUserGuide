| Setting | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Branch Name** | Your company name in Ordflow. Click the pencil icon to change it. |
| **Timezone** | Your local time zone. |
| **Currency** | The currency your order values are shown in. USD by default. |
| **Week starts on** | The first day of the week, for example in reports. |
| **Length Unit / Weight Unit** | The units for package size and weight (CM and KG by default). |

Click **Save** after changing the branch name, time zone, currency or week start.
