## Connect your store

1. Click **+ Add Channel**.
2. Choose your sales channel.
3. You're taken to your sales channel's website. Sign in and allow Ordflow to access your shop.
4. You're returned to Ordflow, and the shop appears in the list with the date it was connected.

![Choosing a sales channel](../assets/img/setup/add-channel.jpg)

Available sales channels: Etsy, Shopify, WIX, eBay, Amazon, WooCommerce, Magento 2, BigCommerce, Squarespace and Walmart.

You can connect more than one shop.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Update** | Refreshes the connection to your shop in the background. Use it if orders stop coming in. |
| **×** | Disconnects the shop. Orders already in Ordflow stay there. |

## Your own courier account

If you have your **own account** with a carrier (for example your own FedEx or DHL account), connect it under **+ Add Courier**. If you ship with Mailog's services, you don't need this.

![Choosing a courier](../assets/img/setup/add-courier.jpg)

Available couriers: DHL, FedEx, UPS, USPS, Israel Post, Stamps.com, Cheetah, UPS Israel and Ordflow USPS.
