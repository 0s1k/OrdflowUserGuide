## What's in the panel

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Order details** | **ID** (your order number), **Order Date** and **Due Date**. |
| **Totals** | **Items Total**, **Shipping**, **Discount** and **Total**. |
| **Recipient** | Name, address, email and phone. Click the copy icons to copy them. |
| **Items** | One block per item: **SKU**, **HS Code**, **Price**, **Quantity**, **Weight** and **Variations**. |

To change something, click the **pencil** next to it. To remove an item, click the **×** next to it. To add an item, click **+ Add Item**.

!!! tip "Due date"
    When an order passes its **Due Date**, the date turns **red**.

## Actions

<div class="hotspot-figure" markdown>
![The Actions menu](../assets/img/orders/order-actions.jpg)
</div>

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Complete** | Marks the order as done. |
| **Archive** | Moves the order off the board. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the order. |
| **Copy** | Makes a copy of the order. Use it to **split an order** in two, for example to keep an EU order under the €150 limit. See [Rules](rules.md#split-an-order). |
